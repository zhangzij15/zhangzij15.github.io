👋About

Zijian Zhang received his B.E. and Ph.D. degrees in electronic engineering from Tsinghua University, Beijing, China, in 2020 and 2025, respectively. His research on beyond massive MIMO for future wireless systems spans hardware architectures, signal modeling, performance analysis, algorithm design, and prototyping. His personal interests also include wireless localization and robotics.

He has published more than 30 papers in SCI-indexed journals and EI-indexed conference proceedings, including IEEE JSAC, IEEE TSP, IEEE TWC, IEEE TCOM, IEEE TITS, IEEE WCL, IEEE CL, IEEE ICC, and IEEE GLOBECOM. His work has received more than 3,000 citations on Google Scholar. One article, with more than 1,400 citations, was designated an ESI Hot Paper (top 0.1%); four articles were designated ESI Highly Cited Papers (top 1%).

He co-authored a chapter in Intelligent Surfaces Empowered 6G Wireless Network and is a co-inventor on Chinese and international patent applications. Selected patent records are available on the Publications page.

About

Zijian Zhang received his B.E. and Ph.D. degrees in electronic engineering from Tsinghua University, Beijing, China, in 2020 and 2025, respectively. His research focuses on beyond massive MIMO for future wireless systems, covering hardware architectures, signal modeling, performance analysis, algorithms, and prototypes. He also has a personal interest in wireless localization and robotics.

He has published more than 30 papers in SCI-indexed journals and EI-indexed conference proceedings, including IEEE JSAC, IEEE TSP, IEEE TWC, IEEE TCOM, IEEE TITS, IEEE WCL, IEEE CL, IEEE ICC, and IEEE GLOBECOM. His work has received more than 3000 citations on Google Scholar. One article was designated an ESI Hot Paper (top 0.1%) and has received more than 1400 citations; four articles were designated ESI Highly Cited Papers (top 1%).

He co-authored a chapter in Intelligent Surfaces Empowered 6G Wireless Network and is a co-inventor on Chinese and international patent applications. Selected patent records are listed on the Publications page.

He received the IEEE Signal Processing Society Young Author Best Paper Award (2025), Tsinghua Science and Technology Best Paper Award (2024), the Tsinghua Science and Technology Excellent Paper Award (2024), and the National Scholarship (2019, 2024). In 2024, he received the Special Scholarship of Tsinghua University, awarded annually to 10 students from a graduate student body of more than 40,000. He was included in the World's Top 2% Scientists list compiled at Stanford University in 2023 and 2025.

Demos

Prototype demonstrations in wireless localization, robotics, and embedded systems.

 

Alongside his research on physical-layer signal processing for 5G/6G communications, Zijian Zhang (second from the right in the photo) has a personal interest in robotics.

He has designed and co-designed robotics prototypes and participated in extracurricular academic competitions.

He participated in RoboCup 2019 in Sydney, Australia.

Demo 1: Human-Tracking Vehicle with UWB Localization

 

Designed by Zijian Zhang, XiaoXiang Li, and Tianyu Wang.

The team designed and built a small unmanned vehicle that tracks a person using ultra-wideband (UWB) localization. The vehicle has a three-antenna array connected to a Decawave DW1000 UWB transceiver and an inertial measurement unit (IMU). A wearable tag transmits UWB signals to the vehicle, which estimates their direction of arrival (DOA) to track the person in real time.

Demo 2: UAV Formation with UWB Localization and Inertial Guidance

 

Designed by Zijian Zhang and Cuijie Xu.

The team developed a four-UAV formation that uses sensor fusion for cooperative localization. The UAVs maintain their relative positions, and any member can lead the formation. They communicate over ZigBee; each carries an IMU, a UWB module, an optical-flow sensor, and a barometer. An extended Kalman filter (EKF) estimates their positions cooperatively. Each UAV also has a controller for manual operation.

Demo 3: Medical Robotic Arm

 

Designed by Zijian Zhang.

During his internship at EBOND in Beijing, Zijian Zhang designed and built a flexible robotic arm, including its mechanical structure, circuitry, and control software. The arm provides centimeter-level positioning control. It uses three high-resolution stepper motors powered by a 48 V supply and controlled by an STM32H7 microcontroller. It supports programmed automatic operation and manual operation through a wireless controller.

Demo 4: Networked Sensor Node for Motor Fault Detection

 

Designed by Zijian Zhang.

During his internship at Pi2Star Technology Ltd. in Beijing, Zijian Zhang designed and built a networked sensor node for motor fault detection. The node can operate as part of an Internet of Things (IoT) system. It combines an IMU, magnetic, infrared, and temperature sensors, and Bluetooth connectivity to collect measurements and transmit them to a host computer. When mounted on an industrial motor, it sends operating data to the host for analysis of the motor's condition.

Demo 5: Tinker Household Robot

 

Designed by the Tinker team at Tsinghua University (team members: Jianghao Huo, Han Zhang, Lubin Ye, Haocheng Ma, Cuijie Xu, Zijian Zhang, Tengfei Zhang, Haitian Shi, Xinyu Han).

The Tinker team at Tsinghua University designed and built the robot's mechanical systems, electronics, and software for the RoboCup 2019 @Home League. Tinker navigates and manipulates objects autonomously in domestic environments and provides an interface for user interaction.

🤖Demos

Hands-on projects in wireless localization, robotics, and embedded systems, from sensor integration to robotic control.

 

Alongside his research on physical-layer signal processing for 5G/6G communications, Zijian Zhang (second from the right in the photo) pursues an interest in robotics through hands-on development.

His experience includes independently developed prototypes, collaborative designs, and participation in extracurricular academic competitions.

He took part in RoboCup 2019 in Sydney, Australia.

Demo 1: UWB-Based Person-Tracking Vehicle

 

Designed by Zijian Zhang, XiaoXiang Li, and Tianyu Wang.

This small unmanned vehicle follows a person using ultra-wideband (UWB) localization. It integrates a three-antenna array, a Decawave DW1000 UWB transceiver, and an inertial measurement unit (IMU). The vehicle receives signals from a wearable UWB tag and estimates their direction of arrival (DOA) to track the wearer in real time.

Demo 2: Cooperative UAV Formation with UWB and Inertial Sensing

 

Designed by Zijian Zhang and Cuijie Xu.

This four-UAV prototype uses sensor fusion for cooperative localization and maintains the UAVs' relative positions in formation. Each UAV carries an IMU, a UWB module, an optical-flow sensor, and a barometer; an extended Kalman filter (EKF) combines the measurements for cooperative position estimation. The UAVs communicate over ZigBee. Any member can lead the formation, and each UAV can also be operated manually with its own controller.

Demo 3: Medical Robotic Arm with Wireless Control

 

Designed by Zijian Zhang.

This flexible medical robotic arm supports programmed automatic operation and manual control through a wireless controller. Zijian Zhang developed its mechanical structure, circuitry, and control software during his internship at EBOND in Beijing. The arm provides centimeter-level positioning control. Its three high-resolution stepper motors are powered by a 48 V supply and controlled by an STM32H7 microcontroller.

Demo 4: Networked Sensor Node for Motor Fault Detection

 

Designed by Zijian Zhang.

Developed by Zijian Zhang during his internship at Pi2Star Technology Ltd. in Beijing, this networked sensor node collects measurements for industrial motor fault detection. It integrates an IMU with magnetic, infrared, and temperature sensors and can operate as part of an Internet of Things (IoT) system. Mounted on a motor, the node transmits operating data to a host computer over Bluetooth for condition analysis.

Demo 5: Tinker Household Robot for RoboCup@Home

 

Designed by the Tinker team at Tsinghua University (team members: Jianghao Huo, Han Zhang, Lubin Ye, Haocheng Ma, Cuijie Xu, Zijian Zhang, Tengfei Zhang, Haitian Shi, Xinyu Han).

Developed for the RoboCup@Home 2019 Open Platform League, Tinker combines mechanical systems, electronics, and software created by the Tinker team at Tsinghua University. Its implemented functions include autonomous navigation and object manipulation in domestic environments, together with an interface for user interaction.

Dense-Array Channel Estimation

Observation design: from ice filling to 2D ice filling

2025–2026 · Bayesian estimation · Dense arrays · RIS

Spatial correlation in dense arrays provides prior information for designing pilot observations. These studies develop ice filling as a discrete counterpart to water filling. Two-dimensional ice filling and a hybrid-array variant extend this approach to joint transmit-precoder and receive-combiner design. For RIS-aided links, alternating Riemannian optimization jointly designs receive combiners and RIS phase shifts, with adaptive covariance learning from received pilots. Analysis or simulations support improvements in estimation accuracy under limited pilot resources.

Fluid Antenna Systems

Successive Bayesian reconstruction of spatially continuous channels

2024–2025 · Fluid antennas · Kernel learning · Adaptive sampling

Fluid antenna systems require channel estimates at many candidate positions, making exhaustive measurement costly. The successive Bayesian reconstructor (S-BAR) models the spatial channel as a stochastic process. It uses an experiential kernel to guide measurement selection and reconstruct channels at unobserved positions. The method does not rely on angular sparsity or slowly varying channels. Simulations under matched and mismatched channel models assess the value of this prior information.

Near-Field XL-MIMO

Low-overhead beam training: hierarchical search and Bayesian inference

2023–2025 · XL-MIMO · Near-field codebooks · Beam training

Near-field beam training must resolve both angle and distance, making exhaustive codebook searches costly. A hierarchical approach constructs multiresolution codebooks with the Gerchberg–Saxton algorithm and adapts them to hybrid arrays without additional time-delay hardware. A subsequent Bayesian regression approach uses correlations among codewords to update a Gaussian process posterior. It infers promising beams from a small set of measurements. Simulations evaluate the tradeoff between training overhead and achievable rate.

Holographic and Continuous-Aperture MIMO

Pattern-division multiplexing for continuous-aperture MIMO

2022–2023 · Continuous apertures · Multiuser transmission · Pattern optimization

In continuous-aperture MIMO, current patterns are functions defined over an aperture. Pattern-division multiplexing (PDM) projects these functions onto finite orthogonal bases, converting the functional optimization into a tractable finite-dimensional problem. Block coordinate descent then optimizes the patterns for multiuser sum rate. Simulations show gains over the evaluated baselines.

Electromagnetic information theory and wavenumber-domain processing

2023–2026 · Random fields · Spatial degrees of freedom · Wavenumber representation

Models of spatially continuous apertures must account for electromagnetic fields and correlations at subwavelength separations. A random-field formulation analyzes mutual information between continuous source and destination regions under white and colored noise. A subsequent overview describes holographic MIMO in the wavenumber domain using a spatial Fourier plane-wave decomposition. It connects this representation to multiplexing, channel estimation, and waveform design, and discusses open questions for wideband systems and network-level operation.

Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces

RIS-aided cell-free networks and multi-hop transmissions

2020–2023 · Cell-free networks · Joint precoding · Multihop links

RIS-aided cell-free networks combine distributed base stations with low-cost reflecting surfaces to improve network capacity. A wideband precoding framework jointly optimizes base-station precoders and RIS configurations through fractional programming and alternating optimization. Related studies address energy-efficiency fairness and extend channel models and beamforming design to multihop RIS reflections. Simulations assess the contribution of these reflected paths.

Active RIS: signal modeling, architectures, and wideband beamforming

2022–2023 · Active metasurfaces · Hardware modeling · Energy efficiency

Multiplicative fading limits the benefit of passive RIS links, particularly when a strong direct link is present. Active RISs address this limitation through signal amplification. This work validates an amplification-and-noise model using a fabricated element and jointly designs transmit and reflection beamforming. Related work examines the energy efficiency of subconnected architectures with shared amplifiers. It also studies grouped-subcarrier designs for wideband channels. An overview discusses RIS fundamentals, common misconceptions, and fading-induced limits.

Compact user-specific RIS for uplink transmission

2021–2022 · User-side arrays · Multilayer surfaces · Transmit beamforming

Large antenna arrays at user devices are constrained by size, cost, and power consumption. This work proposes a compact multilayer user-specific RIS that supports phase control and partial amplitude control of the transmitted signal. Beamforming is designed by alternating optimization to maximize the uplink signal-to-noise ratio. Simulations assess the architecture as a compact implementation of a large user-side array.

Localization, Sensing, and Radio Maps

Signal-multiplexing ranging and asynchronous vehicle localization

2019–2024 · Network ranging · Clock-error mitigation · Connected vehicles

Accurate network ranging must account for clock drift without excessive exchanges between node pairs. Signal-multiplexing network ranging reuses transmissions and timestamp relationships to mitigate clock errors while limiting signaling overhead. Extensions use reflected non-line-of-sight signals for joint localization and sensing. They also introduce a network-measuring protocol for relative localization among asynchronous moving vehicles. The studies evaluate ranging accuracy, positioning robustness, and measurement timeliness.

Urban 3D radio-map reconstruction from sparse UAV measurements

2025 · UAV measurements · Gaussian-process regression · 3D radio maps

Three-dimensional urban radio maps support UAV communications, but obtaining dense measurements is costly. This work collects an urban dataset using a UAV and develops a Gaussian process regression framework to reconstruct radio maps from sparse observations. Offline clustering and posterior-guided online point selection identify informative measurements. Experiments on the collected dataset evaluate the reconstruction framework.

Rydberg Atomic Receivers

Atomic reception for wireless communication and sensing

2026 · Rydberg atoms · OFDM experiments · Joint source-channel coding

Rydberg atomic receivers detect radio signals through atomic transitions. This collaborative article reviews their operating principles and recent developments. It presents experiments on OFDM reception and image transmission using quantized deep joint source-channel coding. The discussion covers practical limitations and potential applications in multiband communication, sensing, and energy-efficient wireless systems.

Dense-Array Channel Estimation

Observation design: from ice filling to 2D ice filling

2025–2026 · Bayesian estimation · Dense arrays · Passive & active RIS

In dense antenna arrays, spatial correlation can be exploited directly in pilot and observation design when training overhead is tightly constrained. We introduce ice filling as a discrete counterpart to classical water filling and extend it to two-dimensional ice filling and hybrid arrays for joint transmit-precoder and receive-combiner design. For passive and active RIS-aided links, we further develop alternating Riemannian optimization and joint observation matrix design—paired with adaptive covariance estimation from received pilots—to co-design combiners and surface responses.

Fluid Antenna Systems

Successive Bayesian reconstruction of spatially continuous channels

2024–2025 · Fluid antennas · Kernel learning · Adaptive sampling

Acquiring channel state information across dense candidate ports in a fluid antenna system (FAS) incurs prohibitive pilot overhead if ports are probed exhaustively. Our successive Bayesian reconstructor (S-BAR) models the spatial channel as a stochastic process and leverages an experiential kernel to adaptively select probing ports and infer unobserved port channels, without assuming angular sparsity or slow channel variations.

Near-Field XL-MIMO

Low-overhead beam training: hierarchical search and Bayesian inference

2023–2025 · XL-MIMO · Near-field codebooks · Beam training

Because near-field beams depend on both angle and distance, exhaustive two-dimensional codebook sweeps become impractical for extremely large-scale arrays. We first design multi-resolution hierarchical codebooks via the Gerchberg–Saxton algorithm, adapted directly to hybrid precoding architectures without extra time-delay hardware. Moving from codebook search to statistical inference, we then formulate a Bayesian regression framework that exploits codeword correlations via Gaussian process posteriors to pinpoint the optimal beam from far fewer pilot measurements.

Holographic and Continuous-Aperture MIMO

Pattern-division multiplexing for continuous-aperture MIMO

2022–2023 · Continuous apertures · Multi-user transmission · Pattern optimization

In continuous-aperture MIMO (CAP-MIMO), transmit waveforms take the form of continuous current distributions rather than discrete vectors. Pattern-division multiplexing (PDM) expands these current functions onto finite orthogonal bases, reducing the infinite-dimensional functional design to a tractable finite-dimensional optimization solved via block coordinate descent to maximize the multi-user sum rate.

Electromagnetic information theory and wavenumber-domain processing

2023–2026 · Random fields · Spatial degrees of freedom · Wavenumber representation

Accurate modeling of spatially continuous apertures requires accounting for electromagnetic wave physics and subwavelength spatial correlations. Using a random-field formulation, we characterize the mutual information between continuous transmit and receive regions under both white and colored noise. Complementing this theory, our wavenumber-domain tutorial applies a spatial Fourier plane-wave decomposition to unify multiplexing, channel estimation, and waveform design for holographic MIMO, while outlining open challenges for wideband and network-level deployments.

Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces

RIS-aided cell-free networks and multi-hop transmissions

2020–2023 · Cell-free networks · Joint precoding · Multi-hop links

Combining distributed base stations with low-cost reflecting surfaces offers a practical path to boosting cell-free network capacity. We develop a wideband joint precoding framework that coordinates base-station precoders and RIS phase shifts via fractional programming and alternating optimization, and extend this line of work to energy-efficiency fairness and multi-hop RIS-assisted transmissions.

Active RIS: signal modeling, architectures, and wideband beamforming

2022–2023 · Active metasurfaces · Hardware modeling · Energy efficiency

Passive RIS links suffer severely from multiplicative (“double”) fading, limiting their capacity gains whenever a non-negligible direct link exists. Active RISs overcome this bottleneck by integrating reflection-type amplifiers into the surface elements. Using a fabricated active-RIS element, we experimentally validate an amplification-and-noise signal model and jointly optimize base-station and reflection beamforming. Follow-up studies investigate energy-efficient sub-connected architectures with shared amplifiers, grouped-subcarrier beamforming for wideband channels, and fundamental open problems of RIS-aided 6G networks.

Compact user-specific RIS for uplink transmission

2021–2022 · User-side arrays · Multi-layer surfaces · Transmit beamforming

Tight size, cost, and power budgets make conventional large antenna arrays impractical on user terminals. We propose a compact multi-layer user-specific RIS (US-RIS) capable of phase and partial amplitude control at the transmitter, paired with an alternating optimization algorithm that maximizes the uplink signal-to-noise ratio.

Localization, Sensing, and Radio Maps

Signal-multiplexing ranging and asynchronous vehicle localization

2019–2024 · Network ranging · Clock-error mitigation · Connected vehicles

High-precision network ranging must compensate for clock offsets and drift without flooding the channel with pairwise packet exchanges. Signal-multiplexing network ranging reuses broadcast transmissions and inter-node timing differences to suppress clock errors with minimal signaling overhead. We further extend this scheme to exploit reflected non-line-of-sight paths for integrated localization and sensing, and to support timely relative positioning across asynchronous connected vehicles.

Urban 3D radio-map reconstruction from sparse UAV measurements

2025 · UAV measurements · Gaussian-process regression · 3D radio maps

Constructing 3D urban radio maps is essential for UAV communications, yet dense aerial field measurements are expensive and time-consuming. Building on a real-world urban UAV measurement dataset, we design a Gaussian process regression framework that reconstructs 3D radio maps from sparse samples by combining offline clustering with posterior-guided online sampling.

Rydberg Atomic Receivers

Atomic reception for wireless communication and sensing

2026 · Rydberg atoms · OFDM experiments · Joint source-channel coding

Rydberg atomic receivers sense radio-frequency fields via quantum atomic transitions rather than conventional RF front-ends. This collaborative magazine article surveys their physical principles and recent advances, demonstrates experimental OFDM reception and image transmission with quantized deep joint source-channel coding, and examines practical hardware constraints alongside prospects for multi-band sensing and net-zero 6G communications.
